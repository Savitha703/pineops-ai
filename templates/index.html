YOUR LOCAL WORKSPACE

Settings

Configuration, privacy, and the details behind your workspace.

Application configuration

Read-only configuration supplied safely by the backend.

To enable AI, set AI_PROVIDER, GEMINI_API_KEY and GEMINI_MODEL in your local .env file, then restart Flask. Your key stays on the backend.

Privacy & responsible analysis

Common passwords, tokens, API keys, connection credentials, and private keys are redacted before analysis. Redaction is best effort: review logs yourself for personal or proprietary data.

When configured, redacted logs and preliminary findings are sent to the selected AI provider. Without a usable AI response, the application explicitly uses rule-based analysis. Saved JSON reports contain redacted patterns and findings, not the original log file.

Severity is a preliminary suggestion. Verify customer impact and service health. Hypotheses are not confirmed root causes. PineOps AI never executes remediation commands.

This is a single-user local demonstration with JSON storage. Keep it on localhost; authentication and production hosting are outside its scope.
